# Rahbar 🛡️

### Personal Safety & Emergency Assistance App

**Rahbar** is a mobile safety application designed to help **anyone quickly seek assistance during an emergency or unsafe situation**.

The app allows users to add trusted contacts and send an emergency alert when they are in a difficult or dangerous situation. During an active emergency, trusted contacts can receive the user's **live location** and, where enabled, **live video**, helping them better understand the situation and respond appropriately.

---

## 🚨 Purpose

The purpose of **Rahbar** is to provide a simple and accessible safety solution for everyone.

Whether a person is traveling alone, facing an emergency, feeling unsafe, or unable to communicate normally, Rahbar is designed to help them quickly notify people they trust.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🆘 Emergency Alert

Users can trigger an emergency alert when they need assistance.

The alert is sent to their selected trusted contacts.

### 👥 Trusted Contacts

Users can add people they trust, such as:

* Family members
* Friends
* Guardians
* Colleagues
* Other trusted individuals

### 📍 Live Location Sharing

When an emergency is activated, the user's **live location** can be shared with trusted contacts.

This helps trusted contacts know where the user is during the emergency.

### 🎥 Live Video Sharing

Rahbar is designed to support **live video sharing** during an emergency.

This can provide trusted contacts with additional information about the user's situation.

### 🔔 Emergency Notifications

Trusted contacts can receive notifications when the user activates an emergency alert.

### 📱 Simple & Accessible Interface

The application is designed to keep important safety features easy to access, especially during stressful situations.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

Rahbar is being developed using:

* **Flutter** — Cross-platform mobile application development
* **Dart** — Programming language
* **GetX** — State management and navigation
* **Firebase** — Backend and authentication services
* **Firebase Cloud Messaging** — Push notifications
* **Location Services** — Live location functionality
* **Camera APIs** — Video functionality

> Technologies may be added or changed as development continues.

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Safety

Rahbar is being designed with privacy and user safety in mind.

Location and video information should only be shared according to the user's permissions and the application's emergency workflow.

Users should only add people they trust as emergency contacts.
